Liner notes

Lyrics enclosed

Children's hospitals were part of my life long before they were part of my career.

That's why I do this work. For fifteen years I've helped hospitals and researchers ask for help. And get it.

Here's what I've learned. People don't give to institutions. They give to a kid. A scientist. A reason.

The job is to find that reason. Put it in front of the right person at the right moment. Then ask. Clearly.

Every track on this record is a version of that.

Thanks for listening · J.H.
